FUD

Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt
Summary
  • FUD is a manipulative propaganda tactic that is used to induce fear, uncertainty, or doubt to influence perception amd behavior
  • FUD is common in finance, marketing, politics, and competitive business contexts
  • It uses selective facts, exaggeration, false information, and negative framing to discourage investment, adoption, or trust in a product, company, or idea
  • In the context of GME, anti-GME FUD is used by GME bears for the purpose of deligitimizing GME, to dissuade potential investors from investing in GME, and to encourage existing shareholders to sell their shares
FUD versus Genuine Criticism

FUD is information or narratives which are typically misleading or dishonest, and which often seek to provoke emotional reactions and uncertainty.

In contrast, genuine criticism is rooted in facts, reasoned analysis, and transparency; its goal is to provide an honest evaluation that can inform decisions. Real criticism acknowledges nuance, it acknowledges competing information from competing viewpoints, it admits limitations, and it encourages understanding.
