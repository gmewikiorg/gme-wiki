Warrants

In 2025, GameStop issued a dividend of warrants with a $32 exercise price
Summary
  • On September 9, 2025, GameStop announced the dividend of warrants to shareholders
    • 1 warrant issued for every 10 shares of GME held on the record date of October 3, 2025
  • Warrant Terms
    • Each warrant allows for the purchase of 1 share of GME at an exercise price of $32
    • The warrants have an expiration date on October 30, 2026
    • The warrants are separately tradable on the NYSE under the ticker GME WS (or GME+ )
  • If all 59M warrants are exercised, GameStop would raise approximately $1.9B
Distribution of Warrants

The dividend of warrants were distributed on October 7, 2025, in accordance with those who held shares of GME and those who held convertible senior notes on the record date of October 3, 2025.
