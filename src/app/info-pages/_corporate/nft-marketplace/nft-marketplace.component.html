NFT Marketplace

From 2022 to 2024, GameStop operated a beta NFT marketplace which never left beta stage
Summary
  • In May 2022, GameStop launched NFT wallet for the Chrome web browser
  • On July 11 2022, GameStop announced the launched their public beta NFT marketplace
  • In August 2023, GameStop indicated that it was ending support for their crypto wallet amidst “regulatory uncertainty of the crypto space”
  • In February 2024, GameStop closed their NFT marketplace "due to the continuing regulatory uncertainty of the crypto space."
