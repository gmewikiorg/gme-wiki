NFT Marketplace

From 2022 to 2024, GameStop operated a beta NFT marketplace which never left beta stage
Summary
  • In May 2022, GameStop launched NFT wallet for the Chrome web browser
  • On July 11 2022, GameStop announced the launched their public beta NFT marketplace
  • In August 2023, GameStop indicated that it was ending support for their crypto wallet amidst “regulatory uncertainty of the crypto space”
  • In February 2024, GameStop closed their NFT marketplace "due to the continuing regulatory uncertainty of the crypto space."
Trajectory

In 2022, as the digital wallet and beta NFT marketplace emerged, many GME investors online were excited about the potential of this venture.

As it eventually came to an apparent close, some shareholders continued to believe that there was still future potential for an NFT marketplace.

CEO Commentary in 2026

In 2026, GameStop has initiated an attempt to acquire eBay. In an interview on the All-In podcast, Ryan Cohen provided some commentary on a potential vision for eBay which would include a digital marketplace.

eBay today is the leader in physical items, physical collectibles as an example. And so I would extend that into digital collectibles.
And essentially, if you look at all of these in-game items, AAA titles that people are accumulating: skins, weapons, all of these things, taking eBay and building a marketplace where you can provide liquidity for in-game digital items.
Essentially, it's what NFTs could have -- people thought they were, but ultimately they had no real utility.
