BBBY

BBBY / BBBYQ ticker - formerly Bed Bath and Beyond, now called 20230930-DK-Butterfly-1, Inc.
Summary
  • BBBY has a curious relationship to GME
  • BBBY was one of the stocks that rallied significantly during the Sneeze, thus giving it the 'meme stock' tag
  • Ryan Cohen made a move on BBBY in 2022 and some retail investors followed; Ryan Cohen expressed intent to hold the board accountable
  • On April 23, 2023, Bed Bath and Beyond declared bankruptcy. The BBBY ticker became BBBYQ
  • On September 29, 2023, the stock was delisted, and the company's legal name was changed to 20230930-DK-Butterfly-1, Inc.
  • The bankruptcy proceedings of DK-Butterfly are ongoing and continue to be discussed on social media
