Held by Registered Holders at Computershare

As a publicly traded company, GameStop is unusual because a significant portion of all issued shares are owned by registered shareholders.

Held by Cede & Co on behalf of the DTCC

All shares not held by registered holders are held by Cede & Co on behalf of the DTCC. This includes all shares held in brokerage accounts by retail investors, insiders, institutions, mutual funds, ETFs, and any other types of beneficial ownership.

Share Issuance and Dilution

In 2022, GameStop shareholders authorized the company to issue up to 1 billion total shares outstanding.

In 2024, GameStop completed 3 at-the-market equity offering programs, raising nearly $3.5B by selling 140M shares at an average of around $25 per share.

In the first half of 2025, GameStop completed 2 private offerings of convertible senior notes, raising an additional $4.2 billion, with additional probable future dilution of approximately 140M shares.

In the second half of 2025, GameStop issued a dividend of warrants, and could potentially raise up to $1.9B if all 59M warrants are exercised and turned into shares.

All together, if all of these future possible dilutions are executed, the total shares outstanding would reach approximately 817M shares.

In 2026, GameStop proposed to increase the authorized share count from 1 billion to 2.5 billion. At the annual meeting, shareholders voted 68.7% in favor of this proposal. This significant increase gives GameStop the flexibility it needs for future equity actions, including the potential acquisition of eBay.
